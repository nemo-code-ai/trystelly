THE IMPORTANT DETAILS

Privacy, simply.

When you join the Stelly waitlist, the form collects the information you choose to submit, including your email address and any optional responses. This information is used to manage the waitlist, understand what people need from Stelly, and contact you about early access and relevant product updates.

The waitlist form is hosted by Tally. Your submission is processed by Tally under its own privacy terms. Stelly does not sell your waitlist information. You can request access to or deletion of your waitlist information by contacting the Stelly team through the channel used to invite you or the contact details published with the product.

Only submit information you are comfortable sharing. This notice should be updated with the final operating entity and a dedicated contact email before a public launch.
